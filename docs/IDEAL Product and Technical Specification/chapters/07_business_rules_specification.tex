\chapter{Business Rules Specification}
\section{Canonical Deal Lifecycle}
\begin{center}
draft $\rightarrow$ negotiation $\rightarrow$ pending\_approval $\rightarrow$ approved $\rightarrow$ locked
\end{center}
Other valid states are changes\_requested, rejected, cancelled, and archived.
\section{Rules}
\begin{longtable}{p{0.12\textwidth}p{0.78\textwidth}}
\toprule
ID & Rule \\
\midrule
BR-001 & A deal may have many versions. \\
BR-002 & Only one version can be the current active version. \\
BR-003 & Only an approved version can become locked. \\
BR-004 & A locked version cannot be edited. \\
BR-005 & Any future change after approval creates a new version. \\
BR-006 & A new version is not valid until all required parties approve it again. \\
BR-007 & Users can only access deals where they are creator or participant. \\
BR-008 & Trust counters are updated only by backend logic. \\
BR-009 & Trial limits and paid access are enforced by the backend. \\
BR-010 & Every important action must be written to audit logs. \\
BR-011 & A party can be required or optional; required parties block final approval until they decide. \\
BR-012 & A party invitation can be accepted, declined, expired, or revoked. \\
BR-013 & Approval is valid only for the exact version that was reviewed. \\
BR-014 & Creating a new version invalidates pending approvals on prior non-locked versions. \\
BR-015 & A rejection returns the version to rejected state and requires a new version or explicit resubmission policy. \\
BR-016 & A change request returns the deal to negotiation and records the requested changes. \\
BR-017 & The prior locked version remains historically valid until a new version is fully approved and locked. \\
BR-018 & Company deals require the actor to have company permission and deal permission. \\
BR-019 & Chat messages are visible only to active deal participants and authorized admins. \\
BR-020 & KYC documents are not visible to normal deal participants. \\
BR-021 & Subscription downgrade cannot unlock features already completed, but can block new deal creation. \\
BR-022 & Admin actions cannot directly change trust counters without a backend trust event. \\
BR-023 & Notifications are derived from backend events, not client-side assumptions. \\
BR-024 & Audit logs are append-only and cannot be edited through application workflows. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\section{Approval and Locking Rule}
For IDEAL MVP, \texttt{locked} is the official final state of a version after all required parties approve the same submitted version. The deal may expose \texttt{approved} as an event or transient status, but the implementation must treat the locked version as the immutable source of truth.
