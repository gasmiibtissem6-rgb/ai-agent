\chapter{Project Charter}
\section{Vision}
\projectname{} will become the trusted workflow layer for digital deals and contracts, with transparent approvals and immutable approved versions.
\section{Problem Statement}
Users often negotiate agreements through fragmented messages, documents, and verbal confirmation. The result is weak traceability, unclear approvals, and unsafe post-approval editing.
\section{Goals}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Provide a simple deal workflow for individuals and companies.
  \item Lock approved contract versions and preserve historical versions.
  \item Centralize sensitive workflows inside the backend API.
  \item Give admins audited review and moderation tools.
\end{itemize}
\section{Non-Goals}
The MVP does not include AI drafting, legal template marketplaces, advanced analytics, enterprise contract lifecycle management, or automated multi-country compliance.
\section{Success Criteria}
The MVP is successful when users can create, invite, negotiate, approve, lock, and revise deals with backend-enforced access control and audit logs.
