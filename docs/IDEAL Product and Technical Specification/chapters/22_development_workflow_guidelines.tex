\chapter{Development Workflow Guidelines}
\section{Branch Naming}
\texttt{feature/mobile-auth}, \texttt{feature/api-deals}, \texttt{feature/admin-kyc}, \texttt{fix/mobile-password-reset}, and \texttt{docs/product-specification}.
\section{Commit Format}
\texttt{feat(scope): message}, \texttt{fix(scope): message}, \texttt{docs(scope): message}, \texttt{refactor(scope): message}, \texttt{test(scope): message}, and \texttt{chore(scope): message}.
\section{Daily Update}
Yesterday: work completed. Today: planned work. Blocked by: blockers. Need from: required support.
